The Idea

One hub, a growing catalog of games, and a leaderboard that carries across all of them.

Land on the hub, pick a game, and play solo or spin up a lobby and share a link with friends — no downloads, no account required to get started. Every result lands on a shared profile and leaderboard that carries across every game, so beating a friend at Tic-Tac-Toe today and topping the 2048 board tomorrow become part of the same story. Guest play works end to end; only the leaderboard and profile ask you to sign in.

Highlights

What actually makes it work

🔓

Play now, sign in later

Solo play and full live multiplayer — hosting included — work with zero account. Logging in mid-match transparently upgrades your session without losing your seat.

🧩

One contract, three shapes of multiplayer

A single plugin interface handles solo (2048), live 1v1 (Tic-Tac-Toe), and host-plus-many (Trivia) — proving the architecture holds before adding rules-heavy games like Chess.

📶

Built to survive a bad connection

Every move is re-validated server-side, never trusted from the client — a dropped connection gets a two-minute grace period to reconnect before it counts as a forfeit.
