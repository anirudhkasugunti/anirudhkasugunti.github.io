Cricket Fantasy Draft

Draft night, for your group chat. A private web app that turns a cricket group chat into one live, unforgettable draft night — and gives everyone a squad to obsess over for the whole tournament that follows.

Designed & built end-to-end by Anirudh — product, architecture, and code
Next.js Neon Postgres + Drizzle Ably Auth.js + Google OAuth Vercel
The Idea

One night on the clock. A whole tournament to watch after.

A friend creates a private league for the tournament and drops the invite in the group chat. Once everyone's in, the room runs a live, turn-based snake draft together — every pick exclusive, every squad locked in for the season, so by the time the first ball is bowled, everyone's already got a team to root for and a friend to trash-talk.

Highlights

What actually makes draft night work

🏏

Real, live snake draft

Turn-based, round-reversing order with a per-pick timer and instant turn advancement — picks are final, no undos.

🛡️

Three-tier missed-pick escalation

Miss your turn → makeup shot → catch-up phase → auto-pick. One absent friend can never freeze the room.

⏸️

Commissioner controls

Lock the lobby, re-randomize the draft order, pause and resume mid-draft — all without leaving the flow.
