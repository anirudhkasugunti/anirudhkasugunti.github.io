The Idea

A shared board, a live call, and a race to two lines.

Create an Arena and share the 6-character code, or drop into one that's already open. Once everyone's in, the host starts the match and numbers call one at a time — every card marks the same call at once, since every player's card draws from one shared pool. First operative to complete 2 lines calls Bingo and wins the Arena.
