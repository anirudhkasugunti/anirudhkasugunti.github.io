About Me 👋

Senior Product Manager with seven years split almost evenly between business and product, most recently leading Seller Account Security at Walmart Global Tech after several product and growth charters at Flipkart spanning demand planning, portfolio and pricing, and customer shopping experience. Each move meant learning a new domain from scratch, and what's carried through all of them is a pull toward problems that don't have a playbook yet, and wanting to be the one who builds it.

Outside of work, that same restlessness shows up as Formula 1 weekends, go-karting in whatever city I'm visiting, and a newer habit of slow solo travel.
